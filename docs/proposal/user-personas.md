# User Personas of the Product

## Target Audience

HASAT is aimed at small and medium-scale olive growers in Türkiye who decide when their orchards are harvested. They rely on fixed calendar dates and habit, although a suitable harvest window depends on the olive variety, the season and the production goal. The three production goals concern olives grown for oil. Agricultural cooperatives and olive mills are potential future users.

## Persona 1: Mehmet Kaya, the volume-oriented family grower

- **Occupation:** Small-scale olive grower who sells his oil olives to the local cooperative mill.
- **Demographics:** 58 years old, lives in a village near Ayvalık, Balıkesir. Primary school graduate. Uses a smartphone for calls, WhatsApp and weather forecasts, and rarely uses a computer.
- **Background story:** Mehmet inherited about 350 olive trees and has farmed them for over thirty years. His income depends on how much oil his olives yield, so he wants maximum oil accumulation. He has always harvested when his neighbors do, but in recent warm seasons the fruit ripened earlier than he expected.
- **Challenges:**
  - He chooses the harvest date from habit, with no orchard-level information.
  - Changing seasons make his fixed calendar dates less dependable.
  - He must arrange workers and mill delivery before he knows when the fruit will be ready.
  - He finds agricultural software complicated.
- **What he needs to be able to do:**
  - Enter his orchard location, olive variety and the "oil accumulation" goal in a few simple steps.
  - See a harvest window with a clear start and end date.
  - Read a short, plain explanation of why it was suggested.
  - Get a clear message when his location or variety is not supported.
  - Use the application from his phone's browser.

## Persona 2: Elif Demir, the quality-focused producer

- **Occupation:** Medium-scale olive grower who produces and sells her own early-harvest extra virgin olive oil.
- **Demographics:** 34 years old, lives in Urla, İzmir. Business administration graduate. Comfortable with web applications on laptop and phone.
- **Background story:** Elif left a corporate job five years ago to build an olive oil brand on her family's land. She manages about 1,200 trees in three orchards with different olive varieties. Her customers pay for high-phenolic oil, so she accepts a lower oil yield to harvest earlier.
- **Challenges:**
  - The window that suits her goal is short and differs between her varieties.
  - She trades phenolic content against oil accumulation without orchard-level data.
  - She must book workers and a mill slot in advance, because delays before milling reduce oil quality.
- **What she needs to be able to do:**
  - Get a harvest window for each orchard and variety.
  - Select "phenolic content" as her goal and compare the other goals.
  - See the selected inputs, data dates, forecast issue date and main reasons behind each recommendation.
  - Receive the window before the harvest season.
  - Be told clearly when data are missing or outdated.
